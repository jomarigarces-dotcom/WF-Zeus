Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Walmart Support  
| Client-Mandated Overtime (OT) Recruitment and Cascade 
 
1. Purpose/ Scope 
This procedure outlines the process for managing Overtime (OT) requests triggered by 
a Client Mandate. It is designed to address spikes in volume by ensuring a seamless 
flow of information from the Client to the Real-Time Analysts (RTA), through the Team 
Leads (TL), and finally to the Agents (OT Takers). This applies to all Lines of Business 
(LOB) under the Operations. 
2. Roles & Responsibilities 
Role: 
Responsibility: 
Client 
Initiates the mandate based on volume spikes and SLA 
requirements. 
RTA 
(Real-Time 
Analyst) 
Monitors volume, validates the need for OT. 
Team Lead (TL) 
Cascades the OT requirement to their specific LOB and 
recruits "OT Takers." 
Support Agent 
Volunteers as an "OT Taker" and executes the additional 
hours. 
 
3. Tools / Requirements 
●​ Workforce Management (WFM) Tool: Incontact tool, ECE IRR Realtime 
Dashboard.  
●​ Internal Communication Tool: Cliq, Email, MS Teams.  

ECENTERPRISES GROUP MANILA INC  
33F Robinsons Summit Center, 6783 Ayala Avenue Makati City, 1200  
Metro Manila, 1200, Philippines  
 
 
4. Procedure (Steps) 
Step 1: Receipt of Client Mandate 
●​ The Client or LOB Manager identifies a spike in volume that cannot be handled 
by the current headcount. 
●​ They issue a mandate for OT. 
●​ Action: RTA and higher Operations receives the notification and assesses the 
required headcount to stabilize the queue. 
Step 2: Cascade to Team Leads (TLs) 
●​ Higher operations send a priority notification to all TLs within the affected LOB. 
●​ Action: The message must include the reason (Volume Spike) and the number 
of agents needed. 
Step 3: Internal Team Cascade and Recruitment 
●​ TLs immediately post the OT requirement in their respective team channels. 
●​ Action: TLs solicit "OT Takers." 
Step 4: Roster Collection and Validation 
●​ TLs gather the names of the "OT Takers" and submit the list back to the RTA. 
●​ Action: RTA and/or Operations updates the roster and informs the Client that the 
additional headcount is being deployed. 
Step 5: Execution and Monitoring 
●​ Agents begin their OT. RTA monitors Adherence to ensure OT takers are actually 
in production using the InContact Tool.  
●​ Action: If the volume spike subsides, RTA notifies TLs to cease OT recruitment. 
Notes / Tips: 
●​ Always prioritize agents with high proficiency in the specific queue experiencing 
the spike. 
●​ Ensure agents take their mandatory rest breaks if the OT extends their shift 
significantly.​

5. Exceptions & Escalation 
​
Volume Spike persists: OT is insufficient to clear the queue. Inform RTA to request 
"Cross-LOB" support.  
Queue Monitoring: Monitor Agents that are still on shift and the OT takers to ensure 
100% productivity within the day. ​

6. Records / Outputs 
●​ OT Tracker: A log of all agents who worked the mandate. 
●​ Post-Shift Report: A brief summary showing how the OT impacted the volume

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Walmart Support  
| Operations Schedule Synchronization & Agent Assistance (Schedule 
Changes) 
 
1. Purpose/ Scope 
This procedure ensures that all schedule updates made in the Transition Scheduling 
Sheet are correctly updated in Zoho. Because the scheduling sheet does not 
automatically sync with Zoho, Team Leads must monitor for changes and prompt agents 
to manually request the shift update in Zoho to ensure time-tracking and payroll 
accuracy. 
2. Roles & Responsibilities 
Workforce/RTA: Updates the Master Transition Scheduling Sheet with new 
event-based shifts, meetings, or coverage requirements. 
Team Lead (TL): Regularly monitors the Transition Scheduling Sheet for any updates 
and informs the affected agents to initiate the change in Zoho. 
Agent: Responsible for submitting the "Shift Change Request" in Zoho once notified by 
their TL to ensure their check-in/out aligns with the new schedule. 
 
3. Tools / Requirements 
●​ Transition Scheduling Sheet: The source of truth for Schedules and shift view. 
●​ Zoho: The official tool for time-tracking and attendance. 
●​ Communication Tool: (Email/Cliq) For supervisory notifications. 
4. Procedure (Steps) 
Step 1: Monitor the Transition Scheduling Sheet 
●​ Daily/Weekly, TLs must review the "Monthly Events/Meetings" tab in the 
Transition Scheduling Sheet.

●​ Action: Identify any agents under your supervision whose shift has been moved 
to cover for event attendees. 
Step 2: Validate Against Zoho 
●​ Open Zoho and check the agent’s current assigned shift. 
●​ Action: Confirm if the change from the Transition Sheet is reflected. If not (which 
is typical), take note of the variance to prevent "Late" or "Absent" tags in the 
system. 
Step 3: Direct Agent Notification (The Cascade) 
●​ Contact the affected agent immediately via cliq or any communication platform.  
●​ Action: Inform them of the specific change (e.g., "Shift moved from 8 AM to 10 
AM to cover"). 
●​ Note: This must be done as soon as the change is spotted to ensure the agent is 
aware before their next login. 
Step 4: Manual Zoho Assistance 
●​Pre-Shift Action: Assist the agent in updating their schedule following “Zoho>

Transition Sheet schedule. 
●​RTA review: RTA may review the schedule request and is subject for approval. ​
TL’s are not authorized to approve schedule changes from their cluster. 
Step 5: Confirmation and Log 
●​ Action: Ensure the agent reacts or responds to the notification to confirm they

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Walmart Support  
| Attendance and Log Dispute Management 
 
1. Purpose/ Scope 
This SOP defines the process for handling agent disputes regarding attendance and shift logs. 
The goal is to ensure that all disputes filed in Zoho are verified through a hierarchy of evidence. 
This procedure clarifies when an agent can file independently and when a TL’s "vouch" or 
mandatory approval is required to validate a claim. 
2. Roles & Responsibilities 
●​ Agent: Responsible for identifying discrepancies and filing the dispute in Zoho with the 
highest priority evidence available. 
●​ Team Leader (TL): Responsible for reviewing and "vouching" for disputes only when the 
agent lacks primary visual evidence (Screenshots/Docs). 
●​ RTA Lead: Acts as an escalation point for complex disputes or cases where evidence 
and TL vouching are both missing. 
●​ RTA / HR Team: Responsible for the final processing of the Zoho dispute based on the 
evidence hierarchy provided. 
3. Tools / Requirements 
●​ Zoho (Dispute logs) 
●​ Evidence Hierarchy: 
1.​ IC Timestamp (Primary Priority) 
2.​ Work Stamp (Activity logs/System docs) 
3.​ Attendance Thread (SOS/EOS posts) 
4.​ TL Vouch (Required only if options 1-3 are unavailable) 
4. Procedure (Steps) 
Step 1: Discrepancy Identification The agent identifies an error in their logs and prepares the 
necessary evidence for filing. 
Step 2: Determining Evidence Priority (The "Vouch" Rule) Before filing in Zoho, the 
agent/TL must check for evidence in the following order of priority:
